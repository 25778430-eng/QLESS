Qless, redrawn

A bolder take on the same black, white and yellow brand. Your place in the queue becomes a real ticket, the home screen greets people by name, maps move to Google Maps, and vendors get a proper Account hub. Scroll through, then compare it with the current design.

Open the current design Try the CIPC switch
What's different
  • Sign-up asks for first name, surname, email and cellphone
  • Home screen says "Good afternoon, Lerato" and shows your live ticket
  • Google Maps with wait times on every pin
  • Bottom tab bar with a big centre button: Scan for customers, Walk-in for vendors
  • Vendor sign-up asks for documents (ID, proof of address, bank letter)
  • Admin can switch the CIPC requirement on or off
  • Vendor Account becomes a business hub
  • Two vendor levels: Basic (no documents) and Verified
  • Each staff member gets their own portal with their own Call next button

11 · Vendor AccountA business hub

Shop details, money, documents and your own profile, in one place.

What happens today when a vendor taps Account?

It opens the same page customers see: their own name, notification ticks, light or dark mode, privacy, and one button that says "Go to Sharp Lines". Nothing about the business itself (staff, hours, bank, plan, documents) lives there. It's spread over Setup and other pages instead.

What it would do in the new design

  • Opens the business hub shown in screen 11
  • Shows approval status and whether the plan is paid
  • Holds payouts, bank details and uploaded documents
  • Keeps personal settings in a "You" section
  • Has a switch to book at other shops as a customer

About Google Maps

Google Maps needs a Google Maps API key from your Google Cloud account, and Google requires billing to be switched on there. Google gives a free monthly allowance, which a new app usually stays inside, but a card has to be on file. Once you send the key I'll swap the current map for Google Maps.
